# TailoraHub Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 22 August 2026

TailoraHub provides a tailoring marketplace for customers, tailors and platform administrators. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used and how users can request support or account deletion.

## Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when users register, book services or use TailoraHub:

- Name, phone number, email address and account identifiers.
- Login and authentication information, including OTP verification status.
- Tailor profile details such as shop name, gender, services, pricing, experience, availability, photos, videos and offers.
- Customer booking details such as selected tailor, service, quantity, stitching requirements, delivery date, measurement method and order status.
- Location data used for nearby tailor search, tailor fixed location and booking-specific customer measurement location.
- Payment-related records such as order amount, tax and fee breakdown, Razorpay payment identifiers, payment status and wallet ledger entries.
- Support, dispute, account deletion and feedback information submitted by users.
- Device, browser and technical information needed for app security, troubleshooting and service improvement.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage customer, tailor and admin accounts.
- Show nearby approved tailors and tailor public profiles.
- Process bookings, order tracking, location sharing, delivery OTP and support requests.
- Process secure payments through Razorpay and maintain wallet/accounting records.
- Notify customers and tailors about bookings, approvals, payments, delivery and order updates.
- Prevent fraud, protect accounts, investigate disputes and operate the platform safely.
- Improve TailoraHub features, reliability and user experience.

## Payments

TailoraHub uses Razorpay for payment processing. Payment card, bank, UPI or other sensitive payment credentials are handled by Razorpay and are not stored directly by TailoraHub. TailoraHub stores payment status, payment reference IDs and wallet ledger records needed for order fulfillment and accounting.

## Location Data

Location data is used to find nearby tailors and to share measurement or visit locations between the customer and tailor for a specific booking. Customers can choose whether to allow browser or device location permission. Tailor fixed location is shown to customers when needed for booking fulfillment.

## Sharing of Information

We may share limited information only when needed to provide the service:

- Customer booking and location details may be shared with the selected tailor.
- Tailor profile, services, availability, media, rating and location may be shown to customers.
- Payment information may be shared with Razorpay for payment processing.
- Maps and geocoding requests may be processed by Google Maps services.
- Data may be stored and processed using cloud infrastructure providers such as AWS.
- Information may be shared with legal or regulatory authorities if required by law.

We do not sell user personal information.

## Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion from the Support section inside the app by choosing the account deletion request option. Users may also contact support at support@tailorahub.com. Deletion requests are reviewed to ensure there are no unresolved orders, payments, disputes, withdrawals or legal/accounting obligations.

## Data Retention

We keep account, booking, payment, support and audit records as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal/accounting obligations, resolve disputes and protect platform safety. When deletion is approved, personal information is deleted or anonymized where legally and operationally possible.

## Security

TailoraHub uses HTTPS for data in transit and follows role-based access controls for customer, tailor and admin features. Passwords and OTPs are stored securely using hashing or equivalent protection. Sensitive payment credentials are processed by Razorpay and not stored directly in TailoraHub.

## Children

TailoraHub is not designed for children under 13. Users should not create accounts or submit personal information if they are not legally permitted to use the service.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TailoraHub changes. The latest version will be available at https://tailorahub.com/privacy.
