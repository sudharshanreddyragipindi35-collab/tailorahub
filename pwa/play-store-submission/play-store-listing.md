## Full Description

TailoraHub helps customers book trusted tailoring services and helps tailors manage their work in one place.

Customers can discover nearby approved tailors, view tailor profiles, availability, services, prices, ratings, photos and offers, book stitching services, share measurement locations, track orders, pay securely with Razorpay, confirm delivery with OTP, follow favorite tailors, receive in-app updates and submit feedback after order completion.

Tailors can manage their profile, gender, availability, services, pricing, offers, photos and videos. They can accept booking requests, manage waiting lists, update order progress, view wallet earnings and raise withdrawal requests for eligible wallet balance.

Admins can approve tailors, monitor bookings, payments, disputes and support requests, manage platform settings, review finance data and operate the platform securely.

TailoraHub is designed for local tailoring businesses and customers in India, with a clean marketplace flow, secure payments, location-assisted booking and transparent order tracking.

## Key Features

- Customer, tailor and admin role-based experience.
- Nearby tailor discovery with map-assisted booking location.
- Tailor services, prices, availability, media, offers and profile pages.
- Flipkart-style order tracking from booking to delivery.
- Razorpay payment flow with admin-controlled settlement process.
- Delivery OTP confirmation before order completion.
- Customer feedback and rating after completed orders.
- Support requests, disputes and account deletion request flow.
- Tailor wallet ledger and withdrawal request workflow.
